02 / The Classes

Pick your fight.

Premier fitness, training and mixed martial arts, with personal attention for every student, from day-one beginners to people who actively fight and compete.

Round 1

Muay Thai & Boxing

Our Muay Thai is the base for our striking program. We also integrate boxing and kickboxing so students have well-rounded striking and stand-up abilities. The program is designed for anyone of any age, gender or fitness level. Our students range from beginners on their first day to students who actively fight and compete.

With our head coach and professional fighter, Josh Williams, we are determined to make sure you can not only defend yourself, but get in great shape as well.

Round 2

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu & Wrestling

Our Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu is the base for our grappling program. We also integrate wrestling so students build strong and diverse grappling knowledge.

The program gives everyone the knowledge and ability to defend themselves, sharpens critical thinking in stressful situations, and develops patience. Gi and no-gi, with open mat for extra rounds.

Round 3 · Ages 5+

Youth Martial Arts

Our youth program starts at age 5. Classes are designed to develop coordination, team-building skills and confidence.

Not only are we a bully-proof program, our students also have the chance to compete alongside our adults and become coaches for the younger classes.

Round 4

MMA & Personal Training

Put it all together. Mixed martial arts for adults, plus one-on-one sessions built around your goal: weight loss, sport nutrition, strength and conditioning, or getting fight-ready.

Cardio kickboxing and strength & conditioning classes round out the week.

04 / The Coaches

Charles Dosher

BJJ Black Belt · U.S. Coast Guard veteran

Charles opened his doors to the Holden Beach community in 2011. He served 6 years with the United States Coast Guard in the Maritime Law Enforcement Division. After his stint with the USCG, Charles transferred to civilian law enforcement, where he is still currently full time, and he is a local wrestling coach for Cedar Grove Middle School.

Charles started training Muay Thai, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and wrestling in 2011 under Black Belt Josh Williams. In 2011 he received his certifications for personal training, strength and conditioning coach, kickboxing instructor and sports nutrition. In 2023 he received his Black Belt in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and became a certified yoga instructor.
